How the piñata was made.

The studio went through a lot of names before it landed here: a frog, a duck, a horse. None of them felt like the place. Then the idea of a piñata stuck: something handmade and bright on the outside, with good things waiting inside.

The mark started as a drawing, not a brief. A paper piñata, standing side on, with tissue bands, a big eye and a ribbon tail.

From there it was traced into clean shapes, keeping the drawing’s outline exactly, and then simplified: every band one flat colour, every stray speck removed, the eye a perfect circle. No shortcuts, just a lot of small decisions.

For one-colour use it becomes a solid silhouette with the eye cut out, which still reads at sixteen pixels in a browser tab.

The name is set in Geist, tightened, with one change: the dot on the i is a tumbling square of orange confetti. It’s the smallest detail on the page, and the one that says what Piñata is.

On the site, the piñata bursts as you scroll and leaves the confetti falling. What’s inside is Create, Media and Sport.
